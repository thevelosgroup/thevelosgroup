How it works

Clients book security through Velos. We assess the site, agree the contract and handle all client billing and collections. Verified partner companies deploy the guards, and Velos pays the partner monthly, according to each contract. You manage your contracts and payments from your own partner dashboard.

Our standards (strictly enforced)

  • Registered company with valid registration and tax documents
  • Valid private-security operating licence or registration from the competent authority
  • Valid insurance covering guards and client property
  • Every guard vetted (police clearance or certificate of good conduct), trained, uniformed and ID-carded
  • Guards employed and paid lawfully, including statutory contributions
  • Two verifiable client references and a clean record for directors
  • Zero tolerance for theft, sleeping on duty, absenteeism or conduct that harms the Velos name
  • A signed Velos Partner Agreement before any deployment, including non-circumvention terms

Applications are reviewed by hand. We may visit your premises, call your references and verify documents with the authorities. Incomplete or inaccurate applications are rejected.
