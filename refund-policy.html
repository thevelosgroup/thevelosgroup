Payments, Delivery and Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Amounts are in Uganda Shillings (UGX)
In short: you pay first by MTN or Airtel Mobile Money. Velos holds your money safely. The seller is paid only after you have received and checked your goods. If something is wrong, you tell us and we put it right or refund you.

1. How paying works

Orders from Velos Global Shop, Velos Fashion, Velos Little Stars and the Velos Foods Store are paid in full before we prepare or dispatch them. The delivery fee is added to what you pay. If you do not pay within 3 hours, the order is cancelled automatically so you do not lose your place or block the item for others; you can simply order again.

Bulk and wholesale orders are paid 70% when you order, to lock in your price and stock, and the remaining 30% when the goods are delivered or collected and checked.

Partner sellers on the Velos Marketplace choose their own terms: either you pay first, or you pay on delivery (for smaller orders only). The terms are shown to you before you order. Restaurants may accept cash on delivery or collection; this is shown at checkout. Hotels and venues use the deposit and cancellation terms shown at booking.

Always pay only to the numbers shown on the Velos payment page. Never pay a seller or rider directly for a pay-first order, and never share your PIN with anyone.

2. Velos holds your money until you are satisfied

For Marketplace partner orders, your payment stays with Velos. The seller is paid after delivery once you confirm that you received your goods, or automatically 48 hours after delivery if you have not reported a problem. If you report a problem, the payment is held until we have looked into it.

3. Check your goods on delivery

Please open and check your order when it arrives, in front of the rider where possible: the right items, the right quantity, no damage, and fresh food. If something is wrong, tell the rider and report it to us as soon as you can and within 48 hours of delivery, from your order tracking page or by WhatsApp, with a photo. Reports after 48 hours may not be accepted.

4. When you get a refund

SituationWhat we do
An item is out of stock or the order cannot be deliveredFull refund, including delivery. Or we can offer a replacement if you prefer.
Your order is not deliveredFull refund, including delivery.
Wrong, damaged, faulty or not as describedReplacement or full refund for the affected items after we verify with your photos and the seller. We collect the item at no cost to you.
You cancel before the order is packed or dispatchedFull refund.
You change your mind after dispatchRefund of the goods if the item is unused, sealed and in its original packaging, returned within 48 hours. The delivery fee is not refunded.
Food, fresh produce and perishablesRefunded only if wrong, spoiled or not delivered. Not refundable for change of mind.
Custom-made, personalised or hygiene itemsNot refundable for change of mind. Faults are covered.
Bulk and wholesale ordersIf we or the seller cannot supply, the full deposit is refunded. If the goods were allocated to you and the order is cancelled for your own reasons, the deposit may be kept. Faulty or wrong goods are replaced or refunded.

5. How and when you are refunded

Approved refunds are sent to the Mobile Money number you paid from, within 3 working days of approval. Mobile Money transaction charges, if any, are not refunded unless the problem was our fault.

6. If you disagree

Tell us what happened on your tracking page or by WhatsApp. We listen to you and the seller, look at photos and messages, and give you a decision within 3 working days. If you are still unhappy, contact us through the contact page and a manager will review it.

7. Sellers

Sellers are paid for delivered orders after the protection period described above, minus the agreed commission. Sellers who repeatedly deliver wrong, damaged or late goods, or who cancel accepted orders, may be suspended.
