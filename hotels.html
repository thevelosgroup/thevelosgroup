Book trusted stays with Velos

Velos Stays is the booking service of The Velos Group, Uganda. Search hotels, lodges, guest houses, apartments and event venues, see what is really available for your dates, and book with a clear total and clear cancellation terms. Guests from anywhere in the world are welcome: see prices in your own currency for reference, and manage your booking online with your reference and email.

Questions

How do I book?

Search a destination and dates, choose a room, enter your details and pay the deposit. You get a booking reference immediately.

Can I book from outside Uganda?

Yes. Guests from any country can book. Bookings are charged in Uganda shillings, and other currencies are shown for reference.

What if I need to cancel?

Each property shows its cancellation policy and your refund before you book. You can cancel online from My booking.

Are the reviews real?

Only guests who completed a stay booked through Velos can review it.
