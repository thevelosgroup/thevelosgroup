Questions

How do I order from Clover Restaurant & Cafe online?
Open the menu on Velos Foods, add your dishes, drop your delivery pin and pay by mobile money. The restaurant is alerted straight away.
Can I book a table at Clover Restaurant & Cafe?
Yes. Choose breakfast, lunch or dinner, the date and number of guests. You receive a booking receipt, shared with the restaurant.
How much is delivery from Kyanja?
The fee is worked out from the distance between the restaurant and your pin, and you see it before you place the order.
Is it safe to pay online?
Pay only the official Velos numbers shown at checkout. Refunds follow written rules if the restaurant cannot serve you.
