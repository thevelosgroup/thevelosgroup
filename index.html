ID & Verification Fee — Deducted From Your First Payout

Formal admission to the Velos Global Elite Technician Network includes a one-time UGX 50,000 credential, background-screening & physical ID card processing fee. You pay nothing upfront — it is deducted automatically from your first completed, verified dispatch payout. If your first job doesn't fully cover it, the remainder carries to your next payout.

This fee covers:

  • National ID (NIN) and reference verification / background screening
  • Trade credential and certification review
  • Production and issuance of your physical Velos Technician ID card

Your application is reviewed for free. The fee only ever applies against money Velos already owes you for completed work — never money you send us first.
